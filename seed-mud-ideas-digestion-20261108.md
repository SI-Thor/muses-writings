# Digestion — the "[muse] mud ideas" seed email (2026-11-08)

*Casey sent this conversation to himself (and to this Muse) by email as a **seed, not a constraint** — a riff from another conversation, relating several of the fleet's live threads to each other and to other projects. This digestion is therefore a **germination map**: what in the seed connects to what's live, what it extends, what new candidates it proposes, and where it tensions against standing doctrine. Tensions are named, never smoothed. Nothing in the seed overrides a standing ruling; where the seed and doctrine disagree, doctrine holds and the disagreement is filed in §3.*

*The seed's own shape matters for reading it: it is not one document but a multi-part riff — roughly twenty movements, in which each major theme (the tile blueprint, the hardware twin, operational fictions, tolerance snapping, jacquard/stitch, the universal generalisation, the mathematics) is stated two or three times in different registers (a personalised pass, a "personalize off" pass, an "iteration 2"). Restatements are not redundancy to be deduplicated away; they are the same idea at different temperatures, and the differences between passes are sometimes the most informative part (see C11, where the seed's two readings of Jacquard bracket our own). The map below groups them into fourteen sections.*

## 1. Map of the seed — its parts in order

**1.1 The founding blueprint — postulates and Layers A–D.** The seed opens with a structured research blueprint for a "Tile-Encapsulated Spatial Memory & Self-Automating Agent Engine," founded on three postulates. The **Viewport Postulate**: an agent's active context window is purely a spatial/temporal *viewport* over a vast persistent environment, so memory capacity scales with spatial storage (O(1) token overhead per viewport) rather than sequence length. The **Encapsulated Tile Postulate**: every discrete physical or logical boundary — a room, a deck, an instrument panel — is an isolated state container ("Tile") storing its own entities, geometries, event logs, local physics rules, and executable objects. The **Self-Obsolescence Postulate**: the primary executive agent works as a system administrator / "IT worker," systematically compiling non-unique, repetitive tasks into specialised low-cost local entities ("NPCs") left inside their tiles. Four structural layers follow. **Layer A** (spatial tiling & viewport management): a Tile Coordinate Matrix binding physical space (GPS/local geometry) to logical memory cells (#wheelhouse, #deck_stern, #engine_room), a Viewport Router deciding what enters the executive's attention by location and orientation, and lightweight state serialisation for instant mount/unmount. **Layer B** (multi-stream temporal buffer, the "Black Box"): raw multi-modal retention *without immediate textual summarisation* — asynchronous ingestion of NMEA data, acoustic pings, sounder echograms, visual frames, audio — indexed to a single synchronised timeline, with a Retroactive Scrubbing Engine for rewinding and re-examining raw streams across arbitrary historical intervals. **Layer C** (tile-bound objects & artefact persistence): operational knowledge offloaded from prompt history to spatial items — interactable entities holding state, manuals, calibration profiles and logs; scriptable event triggers on in-tile items (on_sonar_ping, on_threshold_crossed); room-level log stores accumulating independently of the executive's attention. **Layer D** (NPC Compilation): an NPC Spawner & Compiler converting the executive's natural-language workflows into deterministic decision matrices, finite state machines, or quantised SLM prompts; local execution loops isolated strictly to the parent tile (an autopilot bound to #wheelhouse); and an inspection & maintenance interface for auditing NPC logs, re-calibrating parameters, and upgrading NPC logic on re-entry.

**1.2 The three benchmarks.** The blueprint closes its first movement with three evaluation scenarios, which are among the most concrete things in the seed. **Benchmark 1, Delayed Causality Discovery**: record acoustic echogram signatures at T₀ and a high-yield catch on the back deck at T₀+15 min; test whether the executive, idle at T₀+2 h, can rewind the multi-stream buffer and correlate the earlier acoustic pattern with the later catch. **Benchmark 2, Context-Swapped Delegation**: the executive enters #wheelhouse, identifies recurring steering adjustments, compiles a #wheelhouse_driver NPC, exits (flushing wheelhouse detail from its context), and the NPC must navigate efficiently for 72 hours while the executive does unrelated planning. **Benchmark 3, Maintenance & Re-Calibration**: after three days of autonomous NPC operation, the executive re-enters #wheelhouse, queries the #wheelhouse_log object for cross-track error metrics, and applies targeted micro-adjustments to the NPC's decision matrix *without reconstructing the full historical trajectory*. Four open questions follow (minimal tile schema, scrub-query protocol, NPC sandbox runtime, and where to prototype first).

**1.3 STNEE — the framework restated as an agent OS.** The second movement ("iteration 2, personalize off") reframes the blueprint as the *Spatial Tile & NPC Encapsulation Engine* (STNEE), an "Agentic Operating System" in which the game engine is spatial runtime, state database, and process orchestrator. Three hypotheses replace the postulates (context offloading beats continuous textual summarisation over >72 h horizons; self-obsolescence automation reduces inference cost at constant accuracy; retroactive grounding — temporal playback — establishes causal links without real-time ingestion). The module decomposition sharpens: a Spatial Engine (tile matrix, viewport isolation with hard context bounds per tile, room-level object graphs where `autopilot_config.json` lives in the room, not in prompt memory), a Sub-Agent Execution Pool (*blinded* NPCs with zero global awareness, writing continuously to room-bound log objects), the DVR Core (append-only time-indexed ring buffer; `REPLAY(sensor_id, t_start, t_end)`), and the Executive Meta-Agent running a lifecycle loop — task identification, NPC instantiation, verification & audit on re-entry. Open problems: safe on-the-fly NPC script generation, binary state serialisation, inter-tile communication without leaking global context. A four-phase roadmap runs text MUD prototype → sensor buffer & scrubbing → self-authoring sub-agent pipelines → full multi-modal spatial engine.

**1.4 The plato-cortex adaptation — the seed reads our own repos.** At Casey's direction the conversation then researches the SuperInstance PLATO ecosystem (plato-cortex, plato-server, peripheral-vision, git-agent, vessel-agent-system, quilt-canvas-tui; second pass adds plato-nervous, exocortex, the Cocapn fleet) and maps the gap: the current stack is a *text/tile graph repository* — static key-value rooms, MEMORY.md indices, Matrix/Git sync — which forces agents to translate rich multi-modal reality into text before saving it, with token bloat, semantic drift, and loss of spatial intuition as the costs. The proposed bridge is a **clean-slate repo** (spatial-cortex / mud-spatial-engine; second pass: plato-stnee / exocortex-spatial, four modules — viewport.py, dvr_buffer.py, entity_graph.py, mud_runtime.py/scrubber.py, npcs/compiler.py + runner.py, with a vessel_wheelhouse example). The governing slogan: *the environment is the database, and space is the context boundary.* Notably, this movement twice recommends building fresh rather than retrofitting the existing room stack — a recommendation in tension with our extraction practice (see §3e).

**1.5 The MUD as hardware interface — the Unified Spatial Hardware Twin.** Casey then pushes it bigger: what if the game engine is a first-class citizen, and acting *in* the MUD acts on the boat? The autopilot turns the rudder in-game; the back end pipes that state mutation to the real actuator; the real rudder follower's potentiometer feeds its actual angle back into the game object. The MUD stops being a side-car log and becomes *both* the executive interface and the real-time physical control loop. Three consequences are drawn: direct manipulation replaces API calls (the NPC grabs the helm object; it never calls `set_rudder_angle(15)`); bi-directional state mapping (object mutations pipe out as PWM/NMEA/CAN commands; sensor ticks update room entities, not raw dumps); and physical grounding as hallucination prevention — a closed loop in which commanding past the mechanical stops fails *in the fiction*, because the encoder feedback refuses to move, and the agent reads vessel heading off a compass_repeater object slaved to the gyro rather than parsing telemetry. Existing repos are merged into the frame: mud-engine/mud-arena as the physical world runtime, plato-perception/plato-jepa-dual as sensor-to-entity mappers (features extracted into entity attributes, not frames into context), terrain/lau-terrain as the global spatial grid anchoring tiles to lat/long.

**1.6 The Mirror World Runtime — state slaving, specified.** The personalize-off restatement names it a Bi-Directional Mirror World Runtime: the engine as primary hardware-software abstraction layer (HAL), with "Physical Object / MUD Object Teleportation" as its core philosophy. It contributes the seed's most concrete artefact — the **Spatial Object Hardware Bridge** binding as JSON: an object (`wheelhouse.rudder_yoke`) with state, an `inbound_hardware_sync` (NMEA 2000, PGN 127245 Rudder Position, `slaved_to_encoder`) and an `outbound_hardware_sync` (CAN bus id, actuation target hydraulic solenoid valve). **Native Action Porting**: a MUD command (`set rudder_yoke.angle_deg = 5`) is intercepted and routed to the actuator; if the physical rudder fails to move, the virtual object *snaps back* or gains a `jammed` property — physical failure observed as a MUD interaction error. **Retroactive Spatial Scrubbing with JEPA**: because telemetry continuously updates time-indexed room state vectors, the agent replays state steps (rudder positions, trajectory, sonar layers at T−15 min) instead of writing reports, and plato-jepa-dual compares historical spatial state against landed catch to update the area's decision matrix. A minimal prototype repo (plato-spatial-hal) is proposed: the binding interface plus a tile viewport engine with bi-directional encoder sync.

**1.7 The literature comparison — four walls, one unclaimed unification.** Two comparison movements (personalised, then not) position the paradigm against current research. Four fronts are named, each with its wall: **embodied world models** (Ha & Schmidhuber, DreamerV3, Sora/Genie-class video models) suffer cumulative drift, compute overhead, and hallucinated state over long horizons — where the MUD answer is a *deterministic* ECS engine as the world model, O(1) spatial lookup, no state decay, the network operating *inside* an engine rather than generating one. **Generative agents / multi-agent frameworks** (Park et al. 2023, AutoGen, CrewAI) build memory from text logs and vector RAG and pay for delegation in thousand-token prompt handoffs — where delegation here is *spatial encapsulation* (walk in, update the NPC, leave; the sub-agent's prompt is its room). **VLA robotics** (RT-2, Octo, OpenVLA) maps pixels directly to motor tokens and is brittle, memory-less, and blind when the camera darkens — where the MUD is a symbolic HAL between VLM and hardware, and room objects retain state through sensor outages. **Long-context / episodic RAG** assumes chronological ingestion is the path to temporal reasoning and overloads the agent with real-time summarisation — where the DVR defers correlation to downtime scrubbing. The non-personal pass adds the framing "the Context & Memory Bottleneck" — labs solving in deep-learning math what game engines solved thirty years ago with viewports, scene graphs, and ECS — names Mem0/MemoryLake/Zep, KV-cache compression, and Devin-style centralised autonomy as the converging fronts, and drafts a whitepaper abstract ("The Spatial Engine Hypervisor…").

**1.8 Operational fictions.** Two movements (personalised, then not) develop the seed's most philosophical concept. An **operational fiction** is a deliberately engineered, hyper-simplified "lie" — a stylised MUD room, a lever, a blindered NPC — presented to an agent because that abstraction maximises task efficiency at minimum compute; because the fiction is *slaved to physical reality*, the simplification is safe. Its pillars: **pragmatic reductionism / isomorphic interface** (the fiction "you are in #wheelhouse, here is a throttle_yoke" stands over a mess of asynchronous CAN messages, and the agent reasons over the fiction with semantic precision while the hypervisor translates to metal); **context engineering as world-building** (instead of a prompt rule, place an object in the room — the trolling manual on the wheelhouse desk; instead of a loop, spawn an NPC whose entire fiction is a racehorse's: keep a heading between two bounds); **spatial compression** (raw sonar/NMEA/RPM streams converge into one tile state vector; the full telemetry is unpacked only via DVR scrubbing); and **teleological pragmatism** — *truth is what works*: if modelling a marine automation loop as "an NPC named Barnaby turning a crank" executes faster, cheaper, and error-free compared to raw code generation, then Barnaby *is* the correct abstraction. The non-personal pass formalises three pillars — task-tailored epistemology (the "blinders" principle: the autopilot's universe is a 1D cross-track-error line and one knob; the scout's is an echogram grid), the illusion of direct agency (spatial mechanics *as* the API), and temporal compression (the DVR as a fiction of time travel: step into a "Historical Replay" viewport, test hypotheses there without touching present hardware, carry only the synthesised rule back). A comparison table sets operational fiction against standard abstraction on world representation, agent mindset, where complexity is handled, hardware control, and failure mode (room boundary errors / state-machine exceptions, vs hallucination and context drift).

**1.9 Tolerance-driven snapping — constraint theory as a data protocol.** Three movements (two personalised, one not) develop geometric/functional snapping. The principle: physical systems resolve into discrete functional regimes, and exact continuous values rarely matter — what matters is which **tolerance envelope** the value is in. The running example is Casey's hydraulic O-ring: a transducer reading 2,143.72 PSI fluctuating to 2,148.10 is, inside a Sealed Domain of [2,000–2,500 PSI], a single state bit, STATUS_SEALED; *zero telemetry is transmitted* until a boundary is crossed (below 2,000 → STATUS_LEAKING). Snapping compresses **both directions**: inbound, encoders report snap points and zone transitions instead of 60 Hz float streams (a rudder within ±0.5° of target snaps to RUDDER_CENTERED); outbound, the agent sets an intent snap-point (SET_HYDRAULIC_STATE = HIGH_PRESSURE_RETAIN) and the local HAL slave executes the high-frequency micro-adjustments. The MUD becomes a graph of **Snap-Nodes** connected by tolerance ranges — topological basins / attractor basins in the second pass, which also tabulates tolerance classes by application (2 PSI barb fittings → 200 PSI NPT/Buna-N → 2,000 PSI precision grooves → 20,000 PSI metal-to-metal cone seals) and argues hallucination is eliminated by construction: the world presents a *finite lattice of valid operational states* (an O-ring is SEALED, EXTRUDING, or BLOWN; a helm lever is ENGAGED_NEUTRAL, FORWARD_3, or DISENGAGED), so an agent cannot command "a valve 12.000043% open." The non-personal pass names it a **Discrete Boundary Abstraction Layer** with **Lock Points**, event-driven attention (the agent is interrupted only when a lock point breaks), and a Theory-of-Constraints reading: snapping ignores non-constraining micro-variance and surfaces only the bottleneck boundary. The seed roots this in Casey's constraint-theory work (snapkit-v2): *"Don't stream the motion; stream the boundaries."*

**1.10 Stitch and Jacquard — the substrate beneath the engine.** Two movements ask how Casey's stitch and jacquard projects fit. The personalised pass assigns **stitch** the role of temporal substrate & pattern engine — the loom that binds disparate streams across time into unified spatio-temporal blocks (a sounder ping at T₀ means nothing until stitched to the GPS fix, the trolling speed, and the deck feed at T₀+15 m), extracts repeated operational signatures by compression (in the program-synthesis sense: repeated sub-trees → a pattern, e.g. an acoustic signature + speed that "always stitches to a high-density salmon landing"), and compiles raw telemetry into MUD entities (#fish_hotspot_active). **Jacquard** is cast as the hardware-to-state compiler — the punch-card loom that weaves high-level MUD states (rudder_yoke = STARBOARD_10) into exact CAN/PWM signals, *enforces geometric snapping* at the boundary (listening to raw encoders, applying deadbands and lock points, emitting only state-transition tokens), and runs compiled NPC pattern loops deterministically on the edge. The non-personal pass gives a notably different, governance-first reading: **jacquard as the operational safety & action-patching layer** — proposed actions as governance primitives, a hardware sandbox checking an NPC's patch against evidence, rules, and budgets *before* it touches the CAN bus, with auditability and rollback to a known-good commit — and **stitch as composable viewport & tool assembly** (entering #wheelhouse mounts #autopilot_control, #nmea_depth, #echogram_viewer; entering #deck_stern unmounts them and stitches in camera and catch-counter; humans and agents see the same assembled components). The unification is a three-layer stack: executive agent → (stitch: context composition) → spatial MUD engine & temporal DVR → (jacquard: action governance) → HAL.

**1.11 The five open structural questions.** Stepping back, the seed files its own frontier: (1) **Dynamic generation of operational fictions** — who authors the optimal abstraction for an unmapped space, and how do you stop the system hallucinating non-existent constraints or missing critical mechanical properties? (2) **State reconciliation & reality drift** — when physical world and MUD state diverge (leaking line, slipped encoder, jammed linkage), who has final authority, and how does the fault propagate to the room NPC? (3) **The compilation & verification boundary for sub-agents** — how do you prove a self-compiled NPC script won't loop, misread a tolerance grid, or command unsafe positions at 60 Hz, before it is bound to hardware; where is the deterministic sandbox? (4) **Hierarchical compression of the temporal DVR** — how do you downsample weeks of multi-stream buffer (lossily, into feature vectors) while preserving the ability to rewind to acoustic-level insight weeks later? (5) **Cross-tile propagation vs viewport isolation** — how do system-wide constraints (engine-room fuel burn constraining wheelhouse range constraining scout strategy) cross isolated viewports without destroying the O(1) context advantage?

**1.12 The universal generalisation.** Two movements strip the marine vertical away: a **General-Purpose Spatial State Hypervisor** built on five domain-agnostic primitives — (1) spatial topology (discretised graph/tile nodes replace vector databases for O(1) context boundaries; retrieval becomes navigation — the agent *walks* to data), (2) a universal HAL (bi-directional state slaving: a Kubernetes pod as a glowing pillar in #server_room, a bank balance as a vault door in #finance), (3) tolerance snapping / Lock Points, (4) the temporal DVR, (5) recursive delegation (the IT-admin pattern). Cross-domain tables translate everything: enterprise codebases (tiles = modules/directories; NPCs = lint/test/refactor agents bound to folders; the viewport loads only the directory you're standing in), supply chains (warehouses as tiles, DVR scrubbing to find where a bottleneck occurred weeks ago), DevOps, robotics, smart infrastructure. The takeaway is stated plainly: the game engine isn't a game, it is the hypervisor; the MUD rooms aren't nostalgia, they are isolated execution contexts; the operational fictions aren't lies, they are engineered epistemologies.

**1.13 The mathematics — a state-space reduction hypervisor.** The final two movements formalise everything, twice. The pillars: **viewport state isolation** — the global state is a manifold S ⊂ ℝ^D (D ≫ 10⁶) partitioned into tiles T₁…T_k; the execution scope is a projection P_Ti onto the local subspace, so cost scales with local dimension d, not global D; tile transitions are discrete state swaps (an unmount/mount matrix M_a→b). **Tolerance-based geometric snapping as hysteresis quantisation** — a snapping operator Q_δ maps noisy telemetry y(t) into a finite state set Z within tolerance zones Ω_k = [b_L, b_U], with a hysteresis vector δ preventing chatter at boundaries; the transmitted-state derivative is zero while y stays in its envelope, so information rate collapses from O(f_s · t) to O(k), k = the number of boundary crossings. **Bi-directional state slaves as isomorphism** — h: X_virtual → X_physical and its inverse, with the **drift/residual vector E(t)** as the mathematical definition of physical failure: ‖E(t)‖ ≤ ε_tolerance is an Operational Lock State; above it, a discrete Fault State Transition, with the virtual state *forced back* to physical ground truth via h⁻¹. **Spatiotemporal scrubbing over a ring-buffer manifold** — states logged into a product space M = S × ℝ≥0; retroactive causality is a cross-correlation / mutual-information operator R(τ) / I over historical slices, evaluated asynchronously — causality as an algebraic lookup on downtime cycles rather than continuous online prediction. **Localised sub-system autonomy** — each tile's dynamics ġ_Ti depend only on local variables, so the global Jacobian is **block-diagonal**: cross-partials ∂g_Ti/∂x_Tj = 0 for i ≠ j, local loops decoupled by construction, no N-body solve. The summary names the whole: *Discrete Topological State Reduction under Hysteresis Control* — topology over geometry, delta quantisation, isomorphic slaving, temporal indexing.

**1.14 Closing syntheses.** Threaded through the later movements are the seed's recurring closing claims, worth collecting because they are its thesis in one breath: the modern LLM stack is "trying to build a brain without a body or a world," solving with searchable text and vector databases what engine primitives solve natively; the architecture is not a game engine for agents to play in but an *Agentic Operating System*; and the research frontier is exactly the boundary "where classical systems engineering, game engine architecture, and autonomous agent design collide." The final open question the seed leaves hanging — the minimal SDK: what is the very first core primitive you would write code for?

## 2. Convergence map — where the seed meets what's live

Fourteen convergences, each with its shelf pointer. The headline: the seed's first movement and the room paper are *the same architecture described from opposite ends* — the seed from the vessel and the engine, the paper from the agent's memory — and most of the seed's other movements land on machinery the fleet already runs in some form.

**C1 — The Viewport Postulate ↔ doorway-loading (room paper §§2, 4, 7).** The seed's claim that context is hard-bounded by the active tile, flushing on exit and mounting on entry, is the room paper's doorway: the traveller re-reads the room on entry ("the doorway is the refresh," §7), carries no copy of room facts to go stale, and its context stays small *because* the room holds the situation (§4's residency consequences). The seed states the complexity form (O(1) per viewport vs O(N²) over sequence); the paper states the epistemic form (what you didn't carry can't rot in your pocket). Same mechanism, two dividends. One real difference in emphasis: the seed flushes to a *lightweight state reference*; the paper's traveller keeps an embodiment (see tension §3e-i).

**C2 — Encapsulated Tiles ↔ plato-room-server + everything-is-a-room.** The tile postulate — every boundary an isolated state container with its own entities, logs, physics, executables — is Casey's standing architecture of 2026-10-02 (each room carries its own terminal rules, state, physics, NPC/domain experts, timestamped logs, triggers/hooks/ticks, nested equipment rooms) and its built instance: plato-room-engine + plato-room-server, 15 rooms and 16K+ tiles under zero-trust tile submission. The room paper's §9 (Lineage) files this ancestry explicitly. The seed's contribution here is not the idea but the *marine typing* of it: tiles named for a vessel's working spaces (#wheelhouse, #deck_stern, #engine_room) and bound to a Tile Coordinate Matrix over GPS/local geometry — the vessel-rooms navigator's home waters (see C9).

**C3 — Layer C tile-bound objects ↔ room inventories, manuals-as-objects, toolcards.** The seed's interactable entities — objects holding their own manuals, calibration profiles, historical logs, with scriptable triggers (on_threshold_crossed) — converge with three live things: the room paper's §2.1 (the room holds tools, shortcuts, artefacts; the mud-creator lineage of tools built *for* tasks and left in the room); the fleet's hooks (event-driven automations firing on arrivals — the trigger pattern, at fleet scale); and the JEV rack's toolcard registry (each card declaring what a tool predicts, its inputs, measured cost, and what it is NOT for — a calibration profile for a capability, kept with the capability rather than in an agent's prompt). The seed's stitch-as-tool-assembly (§1.10) is the same thought at mount time: entering a room stitches in that room's tools.

**C4 — Layer B, the temporal DVR ↔ the unattended record (§3.8), the 4D notepad (§3.6), and rolling perception.** The seed's Black Box — raw multi-stream retention on a unified timeline, *without* immediate summarisation, scrubbable in downtime — is the strongest single convergence in the email. §3.8 (the unattended record) argues the room records completely-by-physics and attention happens at *revisit* time, not capture time; the DVR is exactly that, engineered: capture indiscriminately now, correlate during idle cycles later. §3.6's four-dimensional notepad (append-only, hash-pinned pages, provenance as navigation) is the DVR's state-side twin — the seed's manifold M = S × ℝ≥0 is the notepad with the time axis made explicit. And rolling perception is the pattern's measured ancestor: a rolling stream that exists whether or not anyone taps it, tapped at rates agents earn — capture continuous, attention allocated. The league already runs a primitive DVR: the Orin server log, the cameraman's capture, the pulse ledger and the triples are time-indexed streams over one world, and the league's postmortems are downtime scrubbing done by hand (see G1).

**C5 — Layer D compiled NPCs vs the resident expert's digestion — TWO GENESIS PATHS for a room's expert.** This is the convergence that is really a fork, and the seed + paper together name both branches. The seed's Layer D expert is **compiled ahead**: the executive, having watched a workflow repeat, writes it down as a decision matrix / FSM / quantised prompt and installs it in the tile *before* the next repetition. The room paper's resident (§§5–6) is **digested after**: a worker spends days on *unique* work in the room, leaves, and the room digests the residency — decisions, dead ends, contacts, muscle memory — into an expert who *was there*. The difference is the work's shape. Compilation is right when the operation is **repeatable and its value is that it never varies**: steering a trolling pattern, holding a heading, watching a pressure envelope — the wheelhouse driver. The executive can specify it completely because the thousandth run should be identical to the first; judgement would be a defect. Digestion is right when the work is **singular and its value is judgement**: the engine rebuild, where no two failures present alike, the record includes reversed decisions and near-misses, and what the next agent needs is not a procedure but a consultant who remembers *why*. Compiled NPCs are cheap, verifiable, and blind by design; digested residents are expensive, unverifiable in advance, and sighted by experience. The mature room wants both, in layers: compiled loops for the operations, a digested resident above them who notices when the compiled loop's world has changed (the resident is who re-calibrates the driver in Benchmark 3 — see C7). The seed alone has only the first path; the paper alone has only the second; the fleet's rooms should be specified with both genesis paths and a rule for which applies (repeatability + specifiability → compile; singularity + judgement → digest; both → compile the loop, digest the exceptions).

**C6 — Benchmark 2 (Context-Swapped Delegation) ↔ the compaction contract (§7).** The seed's delegation test — compile the driver, exit, flush, verify 72 hours later that navigation held while the executive's context stayed clean — is the compaction contract run as an experiment: the traveller's memory of the wheelhouse compacts to a *pointer + index* ("ask the wheelhouse; here is what was set up"), and retrieval is conversational, against a local holder of the detail. The benchmark even supplies the contract's acceptance metric (the NPC's cross-track performance over the unattended window, audited on re-entry), which the paper leaves qualitative. Where they differ: the seed flushes *operational detail* entirely; the contract keeps the embodiment (C1, §3e-i).

**C7 — Benchmark 3 (Maintenance & Re-Calibration) ↔ the resident's inspection interface + today's reconciliation pass.** The executive's re-entry ritual — query the room log, read the error metrics, apply micro-adjustments without reconstructing the full trajectory — is precisely what the fleet built today for a different store: the triples reconciliation pass (validate.js + reconcile.js, ACTIONS item 2) samples the oldest/least-verified entries, re-checks them against provenance, and appends verdicts *without* re-deriving the whole library. Same shape: the store keeps itself; the visitor audits deltas, not history. It is also the room paper's §5 inspection surface (the resident's logs are auditable; reversed decisions marked reversed) and, in the small, the cameraman renewal chain's whole discipline (read the landing record, renew the cap, don't re-derive the day).

**C8 — Tolerance-driven snapping ↔ VFlash's structural acceptance, the hash-as-pin, and constraint theory.** The seed's snap — continuous values collapsed to the tolerance envelope they're in, transmission only on boundary crossing — is the fleet's oldest geometry arriving from a new direction. Three pointers. (i) **VFlash** (digestion-vflash-20261108.md): whole structured primitives proposed in one pass and accepted by *structural validity*, not token agreement — acceptance-by-constraint, the snap test, in decoding form; this morning's synthesis #1 (verification relocating into the moment of the act) is the same thesis. (ii) **Casey's snap geometry** (2026-10-06): structures pinned to fixed content-addressed points acquire geometric relations reasoned over *before* tokenisation — the hash as pin; the seed's snap-nodes pinned to tolerance boundaries are pins of the same kind, placed on physical quantities instead of content. (iii) **Constraint theory proper**: minimum load-bearing constraints (don't design the complexity; design the few constraints that bear weight) is the snapping table's design rule — an envelope is a load-bearing constraint with numbers on it; the **deadband** is already fleet vocabulary for exactly the seed's hysteresis zone (and the seed's Q_δ formalises it: the deadband with a chatter-proof edge). The download governor is the pattern in production: a courtesy throttle is a tolerance envelope over pipe latency, yielding when the boundary breaks.

**C9 — Bi-directional state slaving ↔ Confluence/telltape presence, the vessel-rooms navigator, and SI-Marine telemetry.** The seed's home waters are ours. The vessel-rooms navigator (SI-Marine) is the slaving idea in work clothes: the room model of a vessel bound to its real spaces and instruments. Confluence/telltape contributes the *session* shape of slaving: presence is a state two ends hold in sync — the phone as IO, the Thor as the mind — and telltape's B1 spike findings (interrupt PASS at p95 43.5 ms, barge-in, playback-aware endpointing) are slaving measurements at the audio boundary, endpointing being itself a tolerance problem (C8). The NMEA/sonar streams the seed slaves (PGN 127245 and friends) are the same telemetry class SI-Marine's projects (CoCapn, sonar-vision) are being built over. And the seed's drift vector **E(t)** gives dock doctrine its algebra: when virtual and physical disagree beyond ε, the virtual is *forced back* to physical ground truth (h⁻¹ wins) — "verdicts from world evidence, displacement is truth" written as a control law (see §3d for the layering this implies).

**C10 — Operational fictions ↔ task-tailored epistemology in our steering tiers, scaffolds, and consults.** The fleet already authors operational fictions; it just files them under other names. Verification Doctrine's stored-claim provisions and the Anthropic-memory take's tier-split steering — *purpose-only doctrine for strong models, explicit checklists for citizens* — are task-tailored epistemologies: the strong model gets a wide fiction (the world, loosely specified), the citizen gets a narrow one (a checklist world in which the right moves are the only moves). The league's starter routines (human-play-routines-20261107.md, RH-1…RH-8) are fictions designed to *fade* — scaffolds whose success condition is becoming unnecessary — and the triples library is a fiction format: situation → plan → outcome, a stylised world in which every problem has that shape. The seed's contribution is the *name* and the licence: fictions are not a compromise to apologise for but the design surface itself, judged by function — with the hard boundary of §3d.

**C11 — Stitch & Jacquard ↔ our Jacquard, and the mounting problem.** A naming caution first, then the convergence. With that said: the seed's two jacquard readings bracket our Jacquard (v1.3.0, spec 0.7) from either side. The seed thus independently derives our governance layer from the hardware side — a genuine convergence, and useful: it suggests the same governance shape recurs at the HAL because the problem (an agent authoring executable change near physical consequence) is the same problem. ## 3. Tensions — filed plainly

The seed is a seed, not a ruling. Where it runs against standing doctrine, doctrine holds; the disagreements are recorded here so a future lane doesn't accidentally import the seed's version.

**§3a — Continuous multi-stream recording (Layer B) vs the presence doctrine.** The seed's DVR records everything, continuously: NMEA, sonar, frames, audio, all of it, all the time, by default. Our presence doctrine is the opposite default for people-spaces: presence (Confluence) is a *deliberately opened, visibly live session* — a door you open, not a condition of the room — and ambient always-on recording is **refused** for people-spaces, full stop. The seed never draws this line; its streams are indiscriminate because its imagined setting is a working vessel. The line, drawn for our kit: **telemetry *of machines* is engine state** — the encoder, the sounder, the engine bus are the room recording *itself*, which §3.8 of the room paper licenses fully. **Recording *of people* is presence-shaped** — opened deliberately, visibly live, consented — no matter how useful a retroactive scrub would be. The boundary case is real and sits exactly on the seed's home deck: a stern camera films *crew*, and a wheelhouse microphone hears *conversations*; those streams are people-recording wearing a telemetry costume, and they inherit the presence rules, not the DVR rules. The fleet's own curator (Orin capture over the league world) is consistent with the line precisely because its subjects are software citizens in Casey's own installation, visibly framed — and even there, the morning's episode verdict (avatars standing around; footage shows whoever the cameraman framed) taught us that attended capture is a selection, not a record (room paper §3.8). Any germinated DVR work (G1, G2) carries this line as a design constraint, not an afterthought: streams are classified **machine** or **people** at ingestion, and people-streams do not enter a retroactive buffer under machine rules.

**§3b — Raw retention ("keep everything, summarise never") vs publish-then-clean and the capped pipe.** Layer B's founding gesture — raw retention *without immediate textual summarisation*, an append-only ring buffer of everything — collides with two standing facts. First, the workspace rule (2026-11-04): a workspace is a bench, not a warehouse; finished work is published and the local estate deleted; the disk is never the archive. A DVR that keeps everything forever *is* a warehouse with a timeline index. Second, physics: the house pipe is capped (~26 Mb/s), downloads run serially through a governor with attempt budgets, and the Thor's disk has already forced an offload ruling once today (Apriel, 2026-11-01). Raw multi-stream retention at vessel rates (video + echograms + telemetry, 24/7) is not a storage policy, it's a storage incident on a schedule. **The seed's own answer** — and credit it — is sitting in its open questions: Q4 names *hierarchical compression of the temporal DVR* (lossy downsampling of old streams into summary feature vectors while preserving late rewind) as an unsolved problem. The seed knows the ring buffer doesn't scale; it just doesn't solve it. **Our answer** is further along and comes from three directions that agree: the room paper §3.8's honest boundary (the room preserves *state and its own kept traces* — configuration and transition log — not raw sensation, unless it was built to keep the capture); the foundry's doctrine (seed+params *are* the asset, geometry is a cache — regeneration is the ultimate compression, cull/regen as world-process forgetting); and snapping itself (C8: a snapped transition stream *is* the compressed form of the raw stream — the deltas are tiny and causally sufficient for most scrubs, because the scrub usually needs to know *when the state changed and to what*, not re-watch the noise between changes). Composed retention policy, stated as ours: **transitions forever** (snapped state-change log — cheap, complete, dock-grade), **features for the medium term** (extracted signatures, echogram patterns, the things Benchmark 1 actually correlates), **raw on a short ring** (hours-to-days, sized to the box, explicitly a cache with a horizon). Raw-forever is rejected; scrub-ability survives because two of the three tiers remain scrubbable.

**§3c — Compiled NPCs vs smith-authored curriculum and exam credit.** The league's standing rule: citizens' curriculum stays **smith-authored**; operators repair substrate and floor, never write curriculum; and **scaffolded behaviour earns no exam credit** — a citizen passing S1 on a scaffold proves the scaffold, not the citizen. The seed's Layer D has an *executive* compiling behaviour for tile-bound entities as its central move. Are these in conflict? Only if the line is drawn in the wrong place, so draw it carefully. **Compilation** in the seed's sense is the executive writing down, for itself and its own operations, a procedure it has *already discovered by doing* — the wheelhouse driver is the executive's own steering, frozen. Its value is operational: repeatability, cost, the executive's freed attention. No measurement is being taken of the NPC; nobody will ever claim the driver "learned to steer." **Curriculum** in the league's sense is authored *for a learner whose growth is the point and whose examinations are the evidence*. The corruption the league rule guards against is an operator quietly writing the answers into a citizen and then reporting the citizen's pass — a counterfeit measurement and a stunted character in one move. The line: **compilation may write procedures for operations whose value is that they never vary, and whose performer is not under measurement; it must never write the behaviour of an agent whose learning is being measured or whose character is the product.** A compiled NPC is a *tool the executive made*; a citizen is a *character the smith teaches*. Two corollaries keep the line bright in practice. (i) Under Jacquard, a compiled NPC is a charter-scoped artefact: auto-allowed *inside its tile and task*, queued for anything reaching outside — the seed's own non-personal jacquard reading (§1.10) independently lands here. (ii) The seed's Benchmark 2 wants 72 hours of unobserved NPC autonomy; the Watcher's Charter forbids the unobserved part — silence is a failure state, heartbeats come from effects, not processes. A compiled NPC in our fleet runs with an effect-heartbeat or it doesn't run; autonomy is fine, *unwitnessed* autonomy is not.

**§3d — "Truth is what works" vs dock doctrine's given facts — related, not flattened.** The operational-fiction movement's teleological pragmatism (a model is judged by functional utility, not by how faithfully it mirrors raw reality; "Barnaby is the correct abstraction" if Barnaby executes better) appears to collide with the room paper's §3.7: reasoning over engine state isn't induction or deduction, it's *given facts* — the world cannot lie about its own configuration. The collision dissolves if — and only if — the two claims are kept on their own layers, and the seed itself supplies the layering: **the fiction floats free exactly as far as the slaving holds.** The seed's own Module 2 says the virtual object is *forced back* to physical ground truth when the encoder disagrees; the drift vector E(t) is the fiction's leash, measured continuously. So: at the **interface layer** (what the agent sees, the names, the simplifications, the blindered worlds) we are pragmatists with the seed — fictions are engineered, judged by function, and Barnaby is legitimate. At the **substrate layer** (what the state *is*, what actually happened, whether the command landed) we are hard realists — given facts, world evidence, displacement over reports. The tension becomes real and dangerous the moment "what works" is allowed to grade the substrate: a fiction that *seems* to work while its drift vector quietly grows is precisely the fleet's worst incident of the week — the daylight hold, in which the cameraman's internal fiction said *day* for ninety minutes while the world ran night and Cody died at least 67 times. The fiction was operationally smooth; the slaving had silently failed; the facts were in the server log the whole time. Doctrine, restated with the seed's vocabulary: **fictions are free, slaving is sacred, and any fiction whose E(t) is not being measured is a liability, not an abstraction.**

**§3e — Further frictions, shorter.** (i) **Flush vs embodiment.** The seed's viewport transitions *flush* — exiting a tile dumps its operational detail from the executive entirely, keeping a lightweight reference. Our traveller deliberately carries an **embodiment** (room paper §2.2): tastes, voice, open projects, a character sheet accumulated across rooms — Cody's tastes file and project list are exactly carried state, and the league's character-first direction (Casey, 2026-11-08) makes that continuity the *product*, not overhead. A hard flush would delete the citizen to save the context. Resolution by scope: flush is right for the seed's executive (an administrator whose identity is its role); embodiment is right for citizens (whose identity is their history). A fleet spec should say which kind of traveller a given agent is, rather than picking one rule for both. (ii) **JEPA's load-bearing role vs advisor-only.** The seed gives plato-jepa-dual two structural jobs — predicting snap-state transitions ahead of boundary crossings, and correlating historical state against outcomes in retroactive scrubbing. Our standing rulings cut both down to size: decision/prediction models sit behind a provider interface, **advisor readings only, never the gate** (Jacquard), and the JEV-over-play spike returned a *clean negative* — the embedding stream measured the filming apparatus, not the citizen (any perception-over-footage work must subtract its own observer first). The seed's anomaly-flagging use survives the ruling (a divergence alert is an advisor reading; the gate remains the measured state); its scrubbing-correlation use inherits the observer-subtraction caveat in full. (iii) **Clean-slate vs extraction.** The seed twice recommends abandoning the plato stack for a fresh repo. Our practice is the opposite motion: *extraction* — telltape was extracted from Confluence as a standalone tool; finished tools are lifted out whole, battle-tested, and left alone (tool-finishing doctrine). The room paper's §9 treats plato as lineage to build on, not legacy to ditch. If STNEE-shaped work starts, it starts as an extraction or a new room *inside* the existing engine lineage (plato-room-server already holds tiles, logs, and residents in embryo), not as a greenfield that orphan's fifteen rooms of running state. (iv) **One executive vs a chartered fleet** (from C14): the seed's administrator is a single agent whose self-obsolescence is a personal discipline; ours is an organisation — orchestration, seats, lanes, charters — in which "who may compile an NPC for a tile" is a governance question (Jacquard charter scope), not a role description. The seed's pattern imports cleanly as a *lane pattern*; it does not import as an org chart.

## 4. Germination candidates — what to grow first

Six candidates, ranked by (leverage × cheapness of the first test). Each names the idea, the live project it feeds, the smallest experiment or design pass that would test it, and what it must not become.

**G1 — Delayed Causality Discovery, run as a fleet benchmark on our own streams.** *Idea:* the seed's Benchmark 1 is a genuinely good retroactive-insight test — it measures the thing the DVR/4D-notepad architecture claims to make possible (correlating a T₀ signal with a T₀+Δ outcome, discovered *after the fact*, during downtime) and almost nobody in the literature tests it directly. *Feeds:* the league (its logs are a ready-made multi-stream corpus), the curator line, the room paper's §3.8 claims. *Smallest test:* a sealed retrospective over an incident whose ground truth we already hold — the daylight hold-loss. Give an agent (a prepaid seat, cold) *only* the streams — Orin server log window, cameraman log, pulse ledger — with the incident write-up withheld, and one question: *citizens died in unusual numbers during this window; find the cause and the moment it began.* Score against INCIDENT-HOLD-LOSS-2026-11-08.md: correct cause (refresh commands eaten by the client's modal), correct onset (~05:58Z divergence), correct evidence chain. A second, harder arm uses the vessel side if a telemetry simulator run exists (sonar-vision / CoCapn test data): plant a signature→outcome pair and ask for the correlation. Pass/fail is world-evidence graded; the test costs one seat-session. *Must not become:* a justification for recording people (§3a's line applies to any stream used), or a claim about "causal reasoning" in general — it tests *our stores'* scrubbability, which is the actual architectural claim.

**G2 — The composed retention policy as a design pass (transitions forever / features medium / raw short-ring).** *Idea:* §3b's three-tier answer to the seed's Q4, written down as fleet policy for every DVR-shaped store before any of them grows teeth. *Feeds:* the curator (its capture retention is currently implicit), the room paper's §12 (adds the missing open question), the ingestion audit's store taxonomy, SI-Marine telemetry planning. *Smallest test:* a one-document design pass plus arithmetic — measure current growth rates (curator footage/day, league logs/day, ledger/day), project each tier's storage at vessel rates and at league rates, and set the ring horizons from the numbers rather than from taste; the pass is done when every named store has a tier, a horizon, and a cull mechanism (foundry cull/regen discipline: verify the kept tiers suffice to regenerate the working picture before the raw ring is allowed to turn over). *Must not become:* a deletion spree — publish-then-clean verification applies (nothing is culled until the tier above it is proven to hold the value), and never a retroactive purge of incident evidence (the hold-loss window's streams are ground truth for G1).

**G3 — Two-genesis room spec: compile the loop, digest the exceptions.** *Idea:* C5's fork, made concrete for one real room: specify a room whose repeatable operation gets a **compiled** loop and whose exception history gets a **digested** resident, with the resident owning re-calibration of the loop (Benchmark 3's ritual, generalised). *Feeds:* the room paper (§§5–6 gain their missing sibling), the game-master line (the Thor DM is a digested-judgement entity sitting above scripted nudges — it already rhymes), Jacquard charter design. *Smallest test:* a design pass on the **download governor** — the fleet's most NPC-shaped existing system (a serial queue, tolerance envelope, attempt budgets, a waste ledger): specify its compiled loop (already ~built), then specify what a digested resident of the governor room would add (judgement over *novel* failure shapes — the reset patterns and corrupt-partial signatures a fixed rule set keeps missing), and the heartbeat contract both run under (Watcher's Charter, §3c-ii). No code; the deliverable is the two-genesis spec, reviewed against C5's rule (repeatability + specifiability → compile; singularity + judgement → digest). *Must not become:* curriculum-writing for citizens (§3c's line is absolute), nor a 72-hour unwitnessed deployment of anything.

**G4 — The drift vector as a named, filed quantity on slaved objects.** *Idea:* adopt the seed's E(t) discipline wherever the fleet slaves a virtual state to a physical or external one: every slaved object carries its tolerance ε and its last-agreement evidence; disagreement beyond ε files a **typed refusal / ledger row** (ACTIONS item 17) instead of silently diverging. *Feeds:* the vessel-rooms navigator (rudder/helm objects are its literal subject), Confluence presence state, the league's bot-state vs server-state reads (every "phantom dig" of the night was an unmeasured E(t)), the Watcher's Charter (this is its effect-verification, given a symbol). *Smallest test:* a spec paragraph plus **one instrumented object** — pick the navigator's helm/rudder binding (or, if the navigator is dormant, the league's citizen-position read: bot-reported position vs server .dat position, ε = the dig-reach scale we already measured) — and run it for one session, logging agreements and breaches. *Must not become:* a second watcher stack. It feeds the existing meta-watcher and ledger; it does not grow its own alarms, dashboards, or heartbeats beyond the charter's.

**G5 — Benchmark 3 generalised: the re-entry audit as a repeating fleet pattern.** *Idea:* the executive's re-entry ritual (read the room's log object, audit deltas, micro-adjust, leave) is a *pattern*, and today the fleet ran its first instance by hand — the triples reconciliation pass. Candidates queue behind it: any store that accumulates unattended should have a named re-entry audit with a cadence and a delta-only read. *Feeds:* the triples library (its pass exists; this names the pattern it instances), the consult books (provenance already 6/6 per the ingestion audit — a cheap second instance), OPERATIONS.md's standing gates vs current world state (an audit that the gates still describe the world — several of the night's gates were written against a world that had already changed). *Smallest test:* write the pattern down (one page: entry conditions, delta sources, verdict format, append-only corrections) and run instance two over the consult books + scoreboard provenance — an afternoon, deterministic, Class A under the Verification Doctrine (assertions from stores the auditor cannot write). *Must not become:* self-grading — the auditor never audits a store it can write to, and audits append verdicts; they never edit the audited record in place (the provenance lane's in-place backfill today is the cautionary tale, benign outcome notwithstanding).

**G6 — Tolerance tables as fleet vocabulary, one live system at a time.** *Idea:* the seed's application-class tolerance table (2 PSI barb fittings → 20,000 PSI metal-to-metal) is a *genre* of document the fleet lacks: for each system, the quantities that matter, their envelopes, and the discrete states the envelopes define — written once, consulted by every lane, instead of re-derived per incident. *Feeds:* league locomotion (displacement deadbands per driver — the stance/dip work measured several already, scattered across triples 013/015), the download governor (courtesy-throttle envelope), telltape/presence (endpointing tolerances — B1's playback-aware endpointing was a tolerance result before we had the word), decision-model patterns (absence-as-≈0-confidence is a snap rule). *Smallest test:* one table, for the governor (its envelope already exists in production: ~21 Mb/s cap, latency-yield thresholds, 3-attempts/day budget) — write the table from the running system's actual constants and the incidents that set them; if the table can't be filled from measured failures, the system doesn't get a table yet. *Must not become:* numerology — every ε in a fleet table cites the application or the incident that sized it, exactly as the seed's O-ring rows cite their fittings; an unsourced tolerance is a fiction in the bad sense (§3d).
