## How to navigate

- **Rooms (this atlas)** are the revisiting structure: start here when you return after days away and want the state of a thread before its documents.
- **[INDEX.md](INDEX.md)** is the flat search surface: every document with a grade and a keywords line, plus the shelf's searchability test log. Search lands there; rooms explain.
- **[CONTACTS-AND-SOURCES.md](CONTACTS-AND-SOURCES.md)** is the people/sources layer: who made what we read, where it lives, what we took, our standing verdict — including the Jev/JiRack disambiguation notes.
- Links inside rooms are **cold contacts**: a path plus an annotation saying what the document *concludes*. No content is copied into the rooms; if an annotation and its document ever disagree, the document wins and the annotation is the defect.
